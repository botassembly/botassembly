# Outline style

A heading is a noun phrase of at most six words. No trailing punctuation, no
question marks, no gerunds at the front.

A bullet is one sentence, at most twenty words, and states a fact or a claim
rather than a topic. "Queue drains by Friday" is a bullet; "the queue" is not.

Nesting stops at three levels. A fourth level is a sign the section should have
been split.
