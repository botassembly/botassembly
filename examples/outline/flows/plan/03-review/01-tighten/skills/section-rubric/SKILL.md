# Section rubric

An outline passes when all five hold.

1. Every section has a heading and at least two bullets under it.
2. No section has exactly one child section. A lone child is the parent.
3. No bullet repeats another bullet's claim in different words.
4. Nesting is at most three levels deep.
5. Every open question from the topic appears somewhere, or is written down as
   dropped.
