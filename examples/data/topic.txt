Rough topic for the annual customer conference report.

Attendance was up and the venue was too small for the Thursday keynote; people
stood at the back. The workshop track filled every seat and three sessions had
waiting lists. Catering ran out of lunch on day two. Feedback cards praised the
hallway track more than the talks.

On cost: the venue took most of the budget and travel took the rest, so the
sponsor money covered the evening event and nothing else.

Open questions: do we book a larger venue, do we cut the keynote in favour of
more workshops, and who owns the sponsor relationship next year.
