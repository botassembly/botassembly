## Item 16 (cache discipline), added post-retro

Baseline for 0001–0008: **true by construction so far** — the driver
sends spec-authored bodies and check feedback verbatim, injects no
timestamps or counters into any prompt (the clock feeds only the record),
and ADR 0012's layout work (ticket 0009) is where ordering becomes
mechanically pinned: its goldens must assert byte-identical prefixes
across send-back rounds and the absence of volatile content anywhere but
the tail.
